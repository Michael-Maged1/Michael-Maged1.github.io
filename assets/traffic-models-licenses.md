# Traffic GLB models

- `traffic-tuktuk.glb`: Tuk-Tuk from Grab3D, CC0 1.0. Source: https://grab3d.com/models/vehicles/tuk-tuk/
- `traffic-moped.glb`: Moped from Grab3D, CC0 1.0. Source: https://grab3d.com/models/vehicles/moped/
- `traffic-bicycle.glb`: Mountain Bike from Grab3D, CC0 1.0. Source: https://grab3d.com/models/vehicles/mountain-bike/
- `traffic-bus3d.glb`: City bus from the Kit Cidade Low Poly project, CC0 1.0. Source: https://github.com/kayquer/kit-cidade-lowpoly

The game normalizes scale and orientation at load time and uses these assets for traffic only.
